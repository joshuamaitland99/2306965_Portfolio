Reading Assignment

I was tasked with reading "The Richest man in Babylon", and these chapters were recommended to me, here is my understanding of them:

Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

My understanding

After reading this chapter, I learned that managing money is important, no matter how much you earn. The chapter teaches us to save money, spend wisely, avoid unnecessary expenses and plan for the future. One thing I understood is that we should not spend all the money we get. Even if we can only save a small amount, it is still a good start. I also learned that improving our skills can help us earn more money in the future.

How I can apply it to my life

As a university student, I have expenses such as food(daily meals), transportation and school supplies. Sometimes it can be tempting to spend money on things I want instead of things I need. I can apply what I learned by making a budget and saving a little money whenever I get paid. I also want to keep improving my computing skills so I can have better opportunities when I finish university and start working in software development where I can get a larger salary and balance these expenses better.

The Five Laws of Gold

My understanding

This chapter taught me that having money is one thing, but knowing how to use it is another. I learned that saving money, investing carefully and listening to good advice can help a person build wealth. It also explains that we should not rush into investments just because someone promises us quick profits. To me, this means that I need to be patient, do my research and understand what I am doing before making financial decisions.

How I can apply it to my life

I can use these lessons by being more careful with how I spend on wants rather than needs and save my money for greater purposes. For example, if I decide to invest in a business, I will first learn or do my research about the investment and understand the risks before putting my money into it. I will not just follow because a friend tells me it is a good idea. I also see education as an investment because the skills I gain from studying computing can help me build a better future as the world move to a more digital era.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway from this book is that I do not have to be rich to start managing my money properly. I can start from what nothing and build good behavior over time. The book helped me understand that saving a little, avoiding unnecessary spending and making wise decisions can make a difference in the long run especially as a young adult seeking greatness. One thing I plan to do is keep track of my spending and save a small amount whenever I receive money or get paid. At the end of each month, I will check how much I spent and how much I managed to save. I believe this will help me become more responsible with money while I complete my studies and work toward my future goal of becoming a software developer and trucker.
