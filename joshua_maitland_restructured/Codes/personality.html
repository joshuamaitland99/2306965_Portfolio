My Three Strongest Traits or Hobbies

1. Cooking

Cooking is something I enjoy doing because I like preparing different meals and trying new things in the kitchen. It helps me relax and be creative. I also have to manage my time so I can enjoy cooking while still keeping up with my schoolwork and assignments.

2. Hardworking

I would describe myself as hardworking because I try my best in everything I do, especially when it comes to school. Sometimes assignments can be challenging, but I try not to give up. I keep working until I understand what I am doing because I want to do well in my studies and achieve my goals.

3. Listening to Music

I enjoy listening to music because it helps me relax, especially when I feel stressed or tired from school. Sometimes, after spending hours studying or doing assignments, I listen to music to clear my mind. It helps me feel better and gives me the motivation to continue with my work.

My Hero or Mentor

Name: Marcus Garvey

Background: Marcus Garvey was a Jamaican-born political leader, activist, and national hero who is known for promoting Black pride, unity, self-reliance, and economic independence. He encouraged people of African descent to believe in themselves, work together, and take pride in their identity.

Favourite scene, motto, or quote: My favourite quote from Marcus Garvey is, “With confidence, you have won before you have started.” This quote reminds me that believing in myself is an important part of achieving my goals.

Why they inspire me: Marcus Garvey inspires me because he believed in himself and encouraged others to do the same, even when things were difficult. I admire his confidence, determination, and willingness to stand up for what he believed in. His example motivates me to take my education seriously, work hard, and never give up on my dream of becoming a software developer. He reminds me that with hard work, confidence, and determination, I can create a better future for myself.

My critical Essey

Using AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch Using AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch As a university student studying computing, I believe tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot can make learning easier when used responsibly. To me, using AI as a tool means getting help when I need it while still putting in my own effort and thinking for myself. I can use AI to explain difficult topics, brainstorm ideas, prepare for exams and identify errors in my programming code. Whether I am studying at home or in the library, AI can help me understand concepts I find challenging. UNESCO (2023) explains that AI can support education, but its use should protect human thinking and independence. However, depending too much on AI can affect my critical thinking, creativity and problem-solving skills. The University of Gibraltar (2024) also warns that AI can provide incorrect information and that relying on it too heavily may prevent students from developing important academic skills. Therefore, I should check facts using reliable sources, test my code and make sure I understand the work I submit. To use AI responsibly, I will follow my lecturers’ instructions, protect my personal information and acknowledge AI assistance when required. My personal rule is to try the work myself first and use AI for guidance, not to do everything for me. This will help me develop the knowledge and practical skills I need to become a successful software developer.
